Dune Dash: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 November 2026
Developer: sebaworldgames
Contact: sebaworldgames321@gmail.com. Write to this address with any privacy question or request.

This policy covers the Dune Dash app for Android (package com.sebaworldgames.dunedash), including its Kids mode and Pro mode. You can also read it inside the game: Settings > About > Privacy policy.

The short version

We (the developer) never receive any information about you or your child, and we don't sell any. The game itself sends nothing: the app doesn't request Android's internet permission. Only three things can ever leave the app, and each one only if you choose it: a picture you Share; your Play Games details if you turn on leaderboards in Pro mode (Google's Play Games library in the app then sends your Play Games name and ID, your Pro scores, and Google's own usage and diagnostics data to Google); and your phone's own backup, if you have turned that on. All three are explained below. It has no ads, no analytics of our own, no accounts of our own and no in-app purchases.

What the app stores on your device

The game saves the following on your device, inside the app's private storage (and in your phone's own backup, if you have turned that on; see Device backup below):

  • Game progress: best scores and distances, gems, unlocked characters and colours, missions, badges, level and XP, medals, Daily run results, eggs, and the dinosaurs in your Nursery.
  • Names you give your baby dinosaurs (up to 12 letters). These names are stored on the device and are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Your settings, such as sound, music, vibration, brightness, motion effects and whether reminders are on.
  • If you turn reminders on: the time and text of each upcoming reminder, so it can be shown on time and restored after the phone restarts.
  • When you use Share: one temporary picture of your run in the app's cache. A new share replaces it.
  • Challenge codes (Pro mode): up to five recent codes with the distances run on them.
  • Leaderboards (Pro mode): whether you turned them on, and the answer to the age question, kept only as "may be under 13" or "13 and over" (the birth year itself is not kept).

We (the developer) cannot see or access any of this information.

Permissions the app uses

  • Vibration: short vibrations during play. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Notifications (Android 13 and newer): asked for only if you choose to turn reminders on. The game offers this once when you first play, and you can change it at any time in Settings. Reminders tell you when an egg is ready to hatch and when a new Daily run is ready. They are created on the device; nothing is sent from a server.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): puts your reminders back after the phone restarts or the app updates.

Sharing

At the end of any run you can tap Share. The app makes a picture showing the run's scene, distance, score, gems, dinosaur, world, mode and date (and medals for a Daily run), with a short line of text. It hands the picture to the Android share menu, and it goes only to the app you choose (for example a messaging app). After that, that app's own privacy policy applies. Nothing is shared unless you tap Share and pick an app. The picture contains no names, account details or location.

After a challenge run in Pro mode, the picture and text also carry a 10-character challenge code. The code holds only the course, the level and speed setting, the distance and which dinosaur was used. It holds nothing about you. Typing a code into the game works on the phone alone; nothing is looked up online.

Leaderboards (Pro mode, optional)

Leaderboards are off until you turn them on in Pro mode. Kids mode never shows them. Before they can be turned on, the game asks for your birth year. If you may be under 13, leaderboards stay off on that phone and nothing is sent.

If you are 13 or over and choose to turn them on, you sign in with Google Play Games, a Google service. Signing in links the game to your Play Games profile (your Play Games name and player ID). From then on, at the end of each Pro run the app gives Google Play Games your score for that level's leaderboard (the Daily and Fast boards take Normal and Hard runs, and the Daily board uses distance; challenge runs are not sent). Google Play Games holds your profile and those scores and shows them to other players according to your Play Games profile settings. Google's Play Games library also collects its own usage and diagnostics data. If you choose the Friends view, Google may ask whether to share your Play Games friends list with the game; it is used only to show that view. Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy) applies to that data. To show a leaderboard, the app asks Google Play Games for the names and scores on it; they are shown on screen and not stored. We (the developer) don't receive any of this.

You can turn leaderboards off at any time in Settings; after that nothing more is sent. Scores already sent stay on Google Play Games until you remove them there, from your Play Games profile settings.

Children

Dune Dash includes a Kids mode for young children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. Kids mode has no leaderboards, no sign-in, no challenge codes, no chat and no way to contact other players, and the whole app has no ads and no purchases. In Pro mode, the age question keeps leaderboards switched off for anyone under 13.

Device backup

Android may include the app's saved data in your device backup to your own Google account, if you have turned on backup in your phone's settings. Google handles this backup under your Google account settings. We cannot access it.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device (and in your own phone backup, if that's on) until you delete it:

  • Settings > Data > Reset progress in the game erases all scores, gems, characters, eggs, missions, challenge codes and settings (except the answer to the leaderboard age question), and cancels any scheduled reminders. Scores already sent to Google Play Games are removed there, not in the game.
  • To remove everything, including any scheduled reminders, uninstall the app or use Android Settings > Apps > Dune Dash > Storage > Clear storage.

Because we never receive your data, we have nothing stored about you that we could delete or hand over.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles information, we will update this policy and the effective date above before the change is released.
